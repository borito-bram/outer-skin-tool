Outer Skin Tool

Keeps only the faces of a STEP model that can be seen from outside. Contact faces, hidden parts and internal cavities are removed. Part names, colours and the assembly tree are kept.

STEP file
Covers optional

Close off openings so everything behind them counts as hidden and is removed. Click Draw cover, then click points around the edge of an opening on the model. Use the section cut to reach openings inside. Double-click or press Enter to finish.

    Without plates the opening stays open and shows an empty inside.

    Settings

    Faces you can only see through a gap narrower than this are removed too. Off keeps everything with a line of sight to the outside.
